Short description
Walking navigation that highlights safer routes, live location sharing, and practical safety tools.

Full description
SafeNight helps you choose safer walking routes at night by scoring route-safety factors and providing turn-by-turn walking navigation, live location sharing with trusted contacts, and safety tools such as street-light context and optional crime-data overlays.

Key features:
- Route safety scoring to compare options before you start walking.
- Street-light and crime-context overlays to support route decisions.
- Turn-by-turn walking navigation.
- Live location sharing with trusted contacts.
- Safety Circle tools to help contacts follow your journey.

SafeNight is intended to support personal safety decisions. It does not guarantee safety in any location.
