Full description (4,000 characters max)
<en-GB>
SafeNight helps you plan walking routes at night. Enter where you are and where you are going, and SafeNight shows route options with a safety score for each one, so you can compare them before you set off.

How each route is scored
SafeNight splits every route into street segments and gives each one its own risk estimate. Each estimate combines:
- Street lighting from OpenStreetMap, estimated where lamps are not mapped. It counts for more when it is dark at the time you walk.
- Recent crime from UK police crime data.
- Road type, such as main roads compared with quieter streets and footpaths.
- Nearby activity: open shops, cafes and other places, CCTV cameras, and bus and transit stops, from OpenStreetMap.
- How isolated the street is, based on its distance from main roads and the number of open places nearby.
- How busy the area is likely to be at the time you walk.

The route's score comes from combining its segments. The safety panel shows the weakest stretch of each route, so you can see where the risk is.

Plan for the time you walk
Choose to leave now, leave at a set time, or arrive by a set time. Scores are calculated for the time you choose.

Route planning
- Compare walking routes and their safety scores.
- Turn-by-turn walking directions for the route you choose.
- Save places you use often, such as home or work.
- Share a route with someone by link.

Live location sharing
- Share your live location with contacts who have accepted an invitation to your Safety Circle, while a journey is active.
- Sharing stops when you end the journey.
- Before the app asks for location permission, it explains why it needs location, including in the background.

AI explanations
- Optional summaries explain how a route score was reached. They are generated by AI from route data and can be wrong, so always check your surroundings.

Hazard reports
- Other users can report hazards on the map. Reports are submitted by users and are not checked by SafeNight before they appear.

What SafeNight cannot do
- Scores are estimates based on the data available. They do not guarantee your safety and do not replace your judgement or awareness of your surroundings.
- Crime data may be incomplete or out of date.
- SafeNight is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 999 or your local emergency number.
</en-GB>
